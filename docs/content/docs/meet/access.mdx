A meeting link lets someone join Zentra Meet. Possessing the link does not grant management permissions, and a Calendar participant list is not a meeting admission list.

## Participants and organizers

Participants can join a meeting that has not ended or expired, then use audio, video, chat, and screen sharing. Organizers can also end, reopen, or delete meetings they created.

For meetings created while signed in, the account identifies the organizer. On another device, sign in to the same account. Using the same display name does not establish ownership.

## Keep guest management access

When a guest creates a meeting, the browser stores a management credential. It is separate from the shareable meeting link and does not move to another device when you copy that link.

Clearing browser data, changing browsers, or using private browsing can lose this access. Signing in later does not transfer a guest meeting to your account; the original browser credential remains the basis for management.

Guest meetings expire seven days after creation. Create a meeting while signed in if you need longer-term management and history.

## Choose how to finish

These actions have different effects on participants, links, and records.

| Action         | Current call                                      | Link and history                                                                |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Leave          | Only you leave.                                   | The link remains usable; other participants can continue.                       |
| End meeting    | The organizer ends the call.                      | Further joining is blocked; the organizer can reopen the same meeting.          |
| Delete meeting | The meeting is removed and the room disconnected. | The link stops working, and associated attendance and chat history are deleted. |

## Decide who receives the link

Someone who receives a forwarded link may not be on the Calendar invitation list. Distribute the link through a channel appropriate to the intended participants and check who actually joins.

An end-to-end encrypted meeting's full link also contains its key. Keep the link intact when sharing it and treat it as an access credential. See [Meeting privacy](/docs/meet/privacy).
