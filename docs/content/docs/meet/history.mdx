Meeting history lets signed-in users review meetings they organized. Attending someone else's meeting does not make it part of your managed history. Guest-created meetings do not move into an account when you sign in later.

## Search the information that is retained

Search by meeting code, attendee name, or saved chat content. Use a distinctive word from a message if you remember the discussion, or the meeting code if you remember the room.

History text search does not include unsaved chat from encrypted meetings or the titles of associated calendar events. Find event titles through [Calendar search](/docs/calendar/search).

## Understand repeated use of a link

A meeting link can be used for several calls, with separate sessions and attendance records. Reopening a meeting keeps its code and does not clear the earlier history.

Meeting history and saved chat are not audio or video recordings. Write down decisions somewhere appropriate rather than expecting chat history to recover a discussion that was never sent as messages.

## Check missing history

Confirm that you are signed in to the account that created the meeting, then check whether it was created as a guest or deleted. Deleting a meeting removes associated history. Expired guest meetings are also cleaned up.

For encrypted meetings, missing chat is the retention policy, not a problem with your search terms. See [Meeting privacy](/docs/meet/privacy).
