Zentra Meet supports instant meetings and meetings attached to Calendar events. An event meeting keeps the schedule and call link together, but editing the event and ending the call remain separate actions.

## Choose a meeting type

Choose based on whether you need a planned date, invitations, or ongoing management.

| Type                      | Use it for                                                   | Ownership                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Signed-in instant meeting | An unplanned discussion you may revisit later                | Managed by the account that created it.                                                    |
| Guest instant meeting     | A temporary call without signing in                          | Management credentials stay in the creating browser. The meeting expires after seven days. |
| Event meeting             | A scheduled date, recurring plan, or participant invitations | Managed by the event organizer.                                                            |

## Create a meeting from Calendar

Add Zentra Meet in the event editor when you want to keep a call associated with its schedule.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Confirm the event scope

Set the date, time zone, and recurrence. If you are editing a recurring event, confirm which occurrences you intend to change.

</Step>
<Step>
### Add Zentra Meet

The meeting field creates a link. Each associated event uses one meeting; reopening the editor does not create another room automatically.

</Step>
<Step>
### Save before distributing the link

A meeting created in an unsaved draft is provisional. Canceling the edit can delete it and invalidate a link you already copied. Save the event before sharing the link with participants.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a meeting or its event

Removing the meeting from an event deletes that meeting and invalidates its link. Adding a meeting again creates a new code, which you need to share with participants.

Deleting the associated event also cleans up its meeting. Deleting one occurrence and deleting part of a recurring series affect different ranges, so check the [recurrence scope](/docs/calendar/recurring-events) first.

## Reuse a meeting link

Leaving a call does not end the meeting. After an organizer ends it, participants cannot join until the organizer reopens it. Deleting a meeting permanently removes it and its associated history. See [Meeting access](/docs/meet/access).
