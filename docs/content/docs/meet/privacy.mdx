You can choose end-to-end encryption when starting an instant meeting. This choice also changes chat retention: regular meetings retain chat for the organizer to review, while encrypted meetings do not save chat history.

## Choose what to retain

Consider whether you need chat after the call and whether participants' browsers support encryption.

| Mode                         | Required to join                                        | Chat history                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Regular meeting              | A valid meeting link                                    | Saved chat is available in the organizer's history and search.    |
| End-to-end encrypted meeting | The complete link with its key, and a supported browser | Chat is not saved and cannot be recovered through history search. |

An encrypted meeting is not a meeting without records. The meeting itself, organizer information, and attendance records remain part of meeting management. No saved chat does not mean no retained metadata.

## Keep the key in the link

The encryption key is in the URL fragment, after `#`. Copying only the domain and meeting code removes the key, so participants cannot decrypt the call as intended.

Share the complete invitation link without shortening it by hand or deleting its fragment. Anyone with that full link also has the key, so handle it as a meeting access credential.

## Resolve an encryption error

If the link is malformed or its key cannot be read, ask the organizer to resend the complete link. If the browser lacks the required encryption support, use a supported browser before joining.

Removing the key does not bypass an encryption error. If the group decides to use a regular meeting, the organizer should create a new one and make its chat retention clear to participants.

## Distinguish calendar protection from meeting encryption

Protection of stored Calendar event fields and Meet's end-to-end encryption are separate mechanisms. Adding a meeting link to an event does not establish that the meeting is end-to-end encrypted. Check the meeting mode shown when creating or joining it.
