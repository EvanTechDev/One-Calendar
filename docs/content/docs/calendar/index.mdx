An event in Zentra Calendar can stand alone or belong to a recurring series. Categories organize your calendar; invitations share individual events. Moving an event into a category does not share that category with anyone else.

## Organize by context

Use separate categories for work, personal plans, or a project. You can compare them in one view or hide categories you do not need. Hiding a category does not delete its events or stop a recurring series.

When an event seems missing, check category filters before changing dates or time zones. Search also uses your selected categories. See [Search events](/docs/calendar/search).

## Use a series for a repeating schedule

A weekly meeting or regular class belongs in a recurring series. The series holds the shared rule, while a rescheduled occurrence becomes an exception. Future dates continue to follow the rule without becoming unrelated copies.

Before editing a series, review [Recurring events](/docs/calendar/recurring-events). The scope of an event change and the scope of an invitation to a new participant are separate choices.

## Understand event ownership

You can edit events you create. For an event you are invited to, the organizer maintains its title, time, and recurrence. You can respond to the invitation and choose a category in your own calendar.

A Zentra Meet link attached to an event opens the call. Accepting the calendar invitation does not give you meeting organizer permissions. See [Meeting access](/docs/meet/access).
